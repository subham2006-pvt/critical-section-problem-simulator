Understanding Critical Section

1. Entry Section

A process requests permission to enter the critical section.

2. Critical Section

The process accesses or modifies the shared resource.

3. Exit Section

The process releases the lock so another process can enter.

4. Remainder Section

The process performs its remaining operations.
